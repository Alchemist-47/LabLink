Fair allocation

When more teams want a machine than it can serve, LabLink ranks them with a transparent score and finds the earliest slot before each deadline, on the requested machine, an equivalent one in another department, or by proposing a swap with a lower-priority team. Nothing changes until you apply the plan.

How the priority score works (0–100)
  • Urgency (0–40): closer project deadline scores higher.
  • Project (0–20): capstone and research above club work, above routine coursework.
  • Fairness (0–25): fewer lab hours used in the last 30 days scores higher, so nobody hogs a machine.
  • Waiting (0–15): longer time on the waitlist scores higher.

A swap is only proposed when the other team scores at least 10 points lower, has a later deadline, and can be moved to another slot before its own deadline.
