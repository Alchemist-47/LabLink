Fair allocation

When more teams want a machine than it can serve, LabLink ranks them and finds each one the earliest slot before their deadline. Nothing changes until you apply the plan.

How teams are ranked (score out of 100)
  • Urgency, up to 40: the closer the project deadline, the higher.
  • Project type, up to 20: capstone and research, then club work, then routine coursework.
  • Fairness, up to 25: fewer lab hours used in the last 30 days scores higher, so nobody hogs a machine.
  • Waiting, up to 15: the longer on the waitlist, the higher.

Each team gets, in order of preference: the machine they asked for, the same kind of machine in another department, or a swap with a team that ranks at least 10 points lower and has a later deadline. The swapped team still gets a slot before its own deadline.
